# Women in Blockchain Canada Research Framework

## Repository Charter

**Version:** 1.0.0

## Purpose

The Women in Blockchain Canada Research Framework serves as the canonical research repository for responsible artificial intelligence, blockchain, Web4, digital governance, and public-interest innovation initiatives developed through Women in Blockchain Canada.

The repository is designed to organize research outputs into structured, version-controlled, and machine-readable artifacts suitable for scholarly publication, GitHub collaboration, Zenodo archival, Figshare datasets, and long-term knowledge preservation.

## Vision

Advance Canada's leadership in responsible AI by building an open, collaborative research ecosystem that connects academia, government, industry, nonprofit organizations, and the public through transparent research, reproducible methodologies, and practical implementation frameworks.

## Mission

Support the development, evaluation, funding, commercialization, governance, and education of responsible AI technologies while fostering interdisciplinary collaboration and measurable societal impact.

## Repository Architecture

The repository is organized into modular research collections that include:

- Research publications
- White papers
- Grant strategy frameworks

- Knowledge graphs
- Evaluation rubrics
- Educational resources
- Policy analyses
- Standards mappings

## Publication Strategy

Each major research collection is designed to support:

- GitHub version control
- DOI generation through Zenodo
- Dataset publication through Figshare
- Scholarly citation
- Machine-readable metadata
- Long-term preservation
