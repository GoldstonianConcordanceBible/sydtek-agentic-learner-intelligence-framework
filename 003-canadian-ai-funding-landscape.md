# Canadian AI Funding Landscape

## National Funding Ecosystem for Responsible Artificial Intelligence Research and Innovation

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Document ID:** WIBC-CRAI-003  
**Status:** Foundational Reference  
**Maintained by:** Women in Blockchain Canada

---

# Purpose

This document establishes a structured reference framework for the Canadian funding ecosystem supporting responsible artificial intelligence, blockchain, Web4, cybersecurity, digital identity, advanced manufacturing, supply chain innovation, education, healthcare, climate technology, and public-interest technology.

The framework serves as a living knowledge base for identifying funding opportunities, understanding institutional priorities, mapping research partnerships, and aligning projects with Canadian innovation objectives.

---

# Objectives

The Canadian AI Funding Landscape seeks to:

- identify major funding organizations;
- classify funding mechanisms;
- map strategic research priorities;
- support multidisciplinary collaboration;
- improve grant readiness;
- reduce duplication of effort;
- strengthen commercialization pathways;
- increase responsible AI adoption;
- improve long-term funding sustainability.

---

The ecosystem includes:

- federal government
- provincial governments
- territorial governments
- universities
- colleges
- research institutes
- innovation superclusters
- nonprofit organizations
- foundations
- industry partnerships
- venture accelerators
- multinational collaborations

# Funding Categories

## Basic Research

Supports fundamental scientific investigation without immediate commercial objectives.

Examples include:

- foundational AI
- mathematics
- computer science
- ethics
- social sciences
- public policy

---

## Applied Research

Supports practical research addressing defined challenges.

Examples include:

- healthcare AI
- logistics optimization
- cybersecurity
- educational technology
- digital government
- blockchain infrastructure

---

## Commercialization

Supports moving research toward market deployment.

Examples include:

- startup formation
- licensing
- technology transfer
- proof of concept
- product validation
- scale-up funding

---

## Capacity Building

Supports development of research infrastructure and talent.

Examples include:

- graduate education
- workforce development
- curriculum development
- laboratory infrastructure
- community engagement
- digital literacy

---

## Public Interest Innovation

Supports technologies that improve societal outcomes.

Examples include:

- accessibility
- responsible AI
- environmental sustainability
- Indigenous partnerships
- digital inclusion
- civic technology

# Funding Readiness Levels

Projects should be assessed according to readiness.

## Level 1

Idea Formation

- research question identified
- literature review initiated
- preliminary concept

---

## Level 2

Research Planning

- objectives defined
- methodology drafted
- initial partnerships explored

---

## Level 3

Proposal Ready

- partners confirmed
- budget prepared
- governance documented
- ethics identified

---

## Level 4

Submission Ready

- complete application
- institutional approvals
- supporting documentation finalized

---

## Level 5

Implementation Ready

- funding secured
- project team established
- reporting systems operational
