# Women in Blockchain Canada Innovation Framework

## Inclusive Leadership, Research, Education, and Responsible Technology Development

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Document ID:** WIBC-CRAI-008  
**Status:** Foundational Innovation Framework  
**Maintained by:** Women in Blockchain Canada

# Purpose

The Women in Blockchain Canada Innovation Framework establishes a national model for advancing women’s leadership, research participation, entrepreneurship, education, governance, and technical contribution across artificial intelligence, blockchain, Web4, cybersecurity, decentralized systems, digital identity, and public-interest technology.

The framework is designed to strengthen Canada’s responsible innovation ecosystem by expanding access to research opportunities, grant funding, technical education, commercialization pathways, leadership roles, and international collaboration.

It treats inclusion as an innovation capability rather than a separate or symbolic activity.

---

# Strategic Vision

Women in Blockchain Canada seeks to build a durable ecosystem in which women participate as:

- researchers;
- engineers;
- founders;
- educators;
- policymakers;
- standards contributors;
- investors;
- governance leaders;
- community builders;
- authors;
- technical operators;
- institutional decision-makers.

The framework supports participation across the full innovation lifecycle, from foundational research to public deployment and global standards development.

---

# Mission

Advance responsible AI, blockchain, and Web4 innovation by connecting women with the research infrastructure, partnerships, funding, education, governance authority, and commercialization support required to lead high-impact initiatives.

---

# Core Objectives

The framework seeks to:

- increase women’s participation in AI and blockchain research;
- expand access to grants and non-dilutive funding;
- support women-led startups and social enterprises;
- improve representation in technical governance;
- create stronger pathways into research leadership;
- support interdisciplinary education and workforce development;
- increase participation in standards development;
- strengthen international collaboration;
- improve access to mentorship and sponsorship;
- document measurable ecosystem outcomes.

# Long-Term Objective

The Women in Blockchain Canada Innovation Framework establishes a repeatable model for increasing women’s leadership and ownership across responsible AI, blockchain, Web4, and digital innovation.

Its long-term objective is to create a globally adaptable standard for inclusive innovation in which women are not only participants, but principal investigators, founders, governors, educators, standards authors, infrastructure builders, and long-term stewards of emerging technology.
