# Canadian Research Funding Organizations Dataset

## Canonical Metadata Standard for Funding Agencies, Foundations, and Research Sponsors

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Document ID:** WIBC-DS-012  
**Status:** Foundational Dataset Specification  
**Maintained by:** Women in Blockchain Canada

---

# Purpose

The Canadian Research Funding Organizations Dataset establishes a standardized, machine-readable catalog of organizations that provide financial support for research, innovation, commercialization, education, entrepreneurship, workforce development, and public-interest technology.

The dataset is intended to function as the authoritative organizational reference for the AI Grant Strategy Series, enabling researchers, institutions, governments, nonprofit organizations, startups, and AI systems to identify funding sponsors and evaluate strategic alignment across Canada's innovation ecosystem.

# Long-Term Objective

The Canadian Research Funding Organizations Dataset provides a standardized institutional foundation for Canada's responsible AI and innovation ecosystem.

Its long-term objective is to create a comprehensive, continuously maintained, machine-readable directory of research sponsors that strengthens funding discovery, partnership development, strategic planning, and evidence-based decision-making across the Canadian research ecosystem.
