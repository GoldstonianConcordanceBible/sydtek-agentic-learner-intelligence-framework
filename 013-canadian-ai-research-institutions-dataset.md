# Canadian AI Research Institutions Dataset

## Canonical Metadata Standard for Universities, Research Institutes, Laboratories, and Innovation Centres

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Document ID:** WIBC-DS-013  
**Status:** Foundational Dataset Specification  
**Maintained by:** Women in Blockchain Canada

---

# Purpose

The Canadian AI Research Institutions Dataset establishes a standardized, machine-readable catalog of institutions contributing to artificial intelligence, blockchain, Web4, cybersecurity, data governance, digital identity, advanced computing, commercialization, and public-interest technology across Canada.

The dataset is intended to support research partnership development, grant consortium formation, institutional benchmarking, talent discovery, ecosystem mapping, knowledge graph construction, and AI-assisted collaboration recommendations.

# Institutional Readiness Classification

Institutions may be assigned one or more readiness levels.

## Level 1 — Emerging

Limited activity with growing research interest or educational capacity.

## Level 2 — Developing

Active programs, researchers, or pilot projects with moderate partnership capacity.

## Level 3 — Established

Recognized research programs, laboratories, funding capacity, and institutional governance.

## Level 4 — Advanced

Nationally significant research capacity, strong industry partnerships, and commercialization infrastructure.

## Level 5 — Global Leadership

Internationally recognized research leadership, major collaborative networks, standards influence, and significant implementation capacity.

# Limitations

The dataset may be affected by:

- changing institutional structures;
- inconsistent public information;
- incomplete contact records;
- differing definitions of research capacity;
- outdated laboratory pages;
- limited disclosure of funding or infrastructure;
- self-reported claims;
- unequal visibility among institutions.

Unverified information should be clearly labeled and should not be treated as confirmed institutional capacity.

---

# Long-Term Objective

The Canadian AI Research Institutions Dataset provides a standardized institutional map of Canada's responsible AI, blockchain, Web4, cybersecurity, and digital innovation ecosystem.

Its long-term objective is to make research capacity, partnership opportunities, institutional strengths, and regional innovation infrastructure discoverable through transparent, version-controlled, machine-readable evidence.
