# Canadian AI Researchers and Subject Matter Experts Dataset

## Canonical Metadata Standard for Researchers, Principal Investigators, Technical Experts, and Innovation Leaders

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Document ID:** WIBC-DS-014  
**Status:** Foundational Dataset Specification  
**Maintained by:** Women in Blockchain Canada

---

# Purpose

The Canadian AI Researchers and Subject Matter Experts Dataset establishes a standardized, machine-readable framework for documenting researchers, principal investigators, technical experts, educators, policy specialists, entrepreneurs, and innovation leaders working across Canada's responsible AI ecosystem.

The dataset is designed to support grant development, consortium formation, expert discovery, peer review, advisory board development, research collaboration, standards participation, and institutional knowledge mapping.

The objective is to document expertise rather than rank individuals.

# Collaboration Readiness Levels

## Level 1 — Emerging

Developing expertise with growing research activity.

## Level 2 — Active

Participating in funded research or technical projects.

## Level 3 — Established

Recognized expertise with sustained research output and collaboration.

## Level 4 — National Leadership

Widely recognized within Canada with significant leadership responsibilities.

## Level 5 — International Leadership

Recognized internationally through sustained scholarship, collaboration, standards participation, or major innovation contributions.

# Privacy and Ethical Considerations

Only publicly available or appropriately authorized professional information should be included.

The dataset should not publish:

- private contact information;
- confidential employment information;
- unpublished personal data;
- protected research information;
- sensitive personal attributes unrelated to professional expertise.

# Limitations

The dataset recognizes that:

- professional roles evolve;
- institutional affiliations change;
- publication records expand over time;
- expertise is multidisciplinary;
- public information may be incomplete;
- collaboration interests may change.

Records should therefore be periodically reviewed and updated.

---

# Long-Term Objective

The Canadian AI Researchers and Subject Matter Experts Dataset establishes a transparent, version-controlled, machine-readable representation of Canada's responsible AI research community.

Its long-term objective is to strengthen collaboration, improve grant competitiveness, accelerate interdisciplinary research, and support the development of a connected national innovation ecosystem through discoverable expertise and evidence-based partnership formation.
