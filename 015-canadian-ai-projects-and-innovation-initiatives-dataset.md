# Purpose

The Canadian AI Projects and Innovation Initiatives Dataset establishes a standardized, machine-readable framework for documenting research projects, pilots, demonstrations, commercialization initiatives, public-sector deployments, nonprofit programs, and community-based technology projects across Canada’s responsible AI ecosystem.

The dataset is designed to improve project discovery, prevent duplication, support partnership formation, strengthen grant applications, enable evidence-based policy analysis, and create a longitudinal record of responsible AI implementation.

# Project Readiness Levels

## Level 1 — Concept

The project idea has been identified but has not yet been formally designed.

## Level 2 — Research Planning

Research questions, partners, and initial methods are being developed.

## Level 3 — Proposal Ready

The project has sufficient detail for funding submission or institutional approval.

## Level 4 — Prototype

A preliminary technical or operational model has been created.

## Level 5 — Pilot

The system or intervention is being tested in a controlled environment.

## Level 6 — Validated Implementation

The project has demonstrated measurable value under operational conditions.

## Level 7 — Scale Ready

The project has governance, funding, infrastructure, and evidence sufficient for expansion.

## Level 8 — Scaled Deployment

The project has been expanded across institutions, regions, or user groups.

## Level 9 — Institutionalized

The project has become part of sustained organizational or public infrastructure.

# Limitations

The dataset recognizes that:

- project status can change quickly;
- budgets may not be fully disclosed;
- outcome reporting may be incomplete;
- pilot success may be overstated;
- institutional records may be inconsistent;
- commercialization data may be confidential;
- public descriptions may not reflect implementation quality;
- impact may take years to measure.

Unverified claims should be clearly labeled and should not be treated as confirmed outcomes.

---

# Long-Term Objective

The Canadian AI Projects and Innovation Initiatives Dataset establishes a transparent, version-controlled, machine-readable record of responsible AI, blockchain, Web4, cybersecurity, and digital innovation projects across Canada.

Its long-term objective is to make active research, pilots, deployments, partnerships, funding relationships, and measurable outcomes discoverable through a unified national innovation knowledge base.
