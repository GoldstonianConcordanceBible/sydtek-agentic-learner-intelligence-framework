# Canadian Responsible AI Education and Workforce Development Dataset

## Canonical Metadata Standard for Programs, Courses, Credentials, Training Pathways, Skills, and Talent Development

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Document ID:** WIBC-DS-019  
**Status:** Foundational Dataset Specification  
**Maintained by:** Women in Blockchain Canada

---

# Purpose

The Canadian Responsible AI Education and Workforce Development Dataset establishes a standardized, machine-readable framework for documenting educational programs, courses, credentials, certifications, apprenticeships, internships, fellowships, workforce initiatives, leadership programs, and professional-development pathways across Canada’s responsible AI ecosystem.

The dataset is designed to support talent mapping, curriculum development, grant strategy, workforce planning, employer partnerships, career pathway design, inclusion analysis, institutional collaboration, and long-term national capacity building.

It connects education and training investments with measurable workforce outcomes.

# Education Readiness Levels

## Level 1 — Awareness

The organization provides introductory responsible AI learning.

## Level 2 — Foundational Training

Structured courses or workshops develop basic knowledge and skills.

## Level 3 — Applied Learning

Learners complete practical projects or work-integrated learning.

## Level 4 — Professional Pathway

Programs connect learning to credentials, occupations, and employment.

## Level 5 — Institutional Capacity

Education, research, employer partnerships, and governance are integrated.

## Level 6 — National or International Leadership

The provider contributes to standards, research, policy, and scalable workforce development.

---

# Workforce Development Maturity Levels

## Level 1 — Ad Hoc

Training occurs without coordinated planning or measurement.

## Level 2 — Programmatic

Formal programs and learning objectives are documented.

## Level 3 — Employer Aligned

Curriculum is informed by occupational and employer requirements.

## Level 4 — Outcome Measured

Completion, employment, and skills outcomes are tracked.

## Level 5 — Ecosystem Integrated

Education providers, employers, governments, and communities coordinate pathways.

## Level 6 — Continuously Improved

Longitudinal evidence informs curriculum, funding, policy, and workforce strategy.

# Limitations

The dataset recognizes that:

- program offerings change frequently;
- tuition and fees may change;
- course descriptions may not reflect current delivery;
- employment outcomes may be self-reported;
- credential recognition varies by employer and jurisdiction;
- labour-market demand changes over time;
- public outcome data may be incomplete;
- demographic data may be unavailable or sensitive;
- salary information may be inconsistent;
- participation does not guarantee employment.

Education and workforce claims should be interpreted according to the quality and recency of the available evidence.

---

# Long-Term Objective

The Canadian Responsible AI Education and Workforce Development Dataset establishes a transparent, version-controlled, machine-readable foundation for understanding how Canada develops the researchers, practitioners, leaders, founders, policymakers, and public-interest professionals required for a trustworthy AI ecosystem.

Its long-term objective is to connect education with employment, research, entrepreneurship, governance, and measurable social impact while expanding equitable access to responsible AI opportunities across Canada.
